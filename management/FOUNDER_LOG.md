# Founder Log

**Purpose**: a low-friction journal for capturing anything worth remembering that doesn't belong in a formal spec, decision record, or sprint plan — customer conversations, half-formed product ideas, competitor observations, lessons learned, business ideas, open questions, and future opportunities. The entire point of this file is to let you capture a thought in ten seconds **without interrupting whatever you're currently building**, so good ideas don't get lost just because they showed up at an inconvenient time.

**How to use this**: add a dated entry under the relevant section whenever something worth remembering happens. Entries can be as short as one line. Do not edit this file to "clean it up" or make it read well — it's a journal, not a deliverable. If an idea here turns into something real (a backlog item or a decision), move a reference to it into the appropriate file ([`BACKLOG.md`](./BACKLOG.md), [`DECISIONS.md`](./DECISIONS.md)) and leave the original journal entry in place as history.

**Maintenance rule**: there isn't one, beyond "write things down as they happen." This file is allowed to be messy.

## Lessons Learned

*What didn't work, what took longer than expected, what you'd do differently — the kind of thing that's obvious in hindsight and easy to forget.*

- **2026-07-23** — The original "under two minutes" promise, combined with fully removing the granular checklist from V1, was a reasonable-sounding recommendation that the founder correctly rejected in favor of a progressive model (Quick Appraisal + optional Detailed Inspection on one data model). Lesson: a clean, simple-sounding trade-off ("just make it fast, defer the rest") can quietly throw away real product value if it isn't checked against what the founder actually wants to build, not just what's easiest to specify. Worth remembering the next time a "simpler" option is proposed as a default.

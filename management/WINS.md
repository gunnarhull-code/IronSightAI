# Wins — Milestone Journal

**Purpose**: a record of progress worth celebrating, not just tracking. Building a company is long and mostly consists of unglamorous work — this file exists so that real milestones, however small they might feel in the moment, are actually marked as the achievements they are, rather than immediately forgotten in the rush to the next task.

**Maintenance rule**: whenever a milestone is hit — from the list below or a new one that comes up — add it here with the date, and take a moment to actually acknowledge it before moving to the next task. This file only ever grows; nothing gets removed from it.
